# UrbanHue Position in the Current Research System

## Status

UrbanHue is a previous major project and is currently treated as:

1. archived research context;
2. review-response and revision support;
3. methodological memory;
4. optional inspiration for scene-level landmark discovery.

UrbanHue should not be the protagonist of the new navigational landmarkness / cognitive map project.

## Why UrbanHue is retained

The UrbanHue paper is still in the submission/review lifecycle. Future reviewer responses, revisions, code clarification, and figure refinement may require detailed knowledge of its pipeline, claims, figures, and settled decisions.

## UrbanHue core idea

UrbanHue maps high-dimensional urban features to a perceptually meaningful continuous color field through dimensionality reduction and CIELAB fitting, making high-dimensional similarity visible in geographic space.

The central insight is similarity visibility:

- discrete clustering shows category membership;
- continuous perceptual color fields show degrees of similarity;
- this makes high-dimensional urban feature similarity directly inspectable on maps.

## What can transfer

- Scene-level or atmospheric landmarks may be discovered from high-dimensional visual similarity.
- Local visual evidence should be linked to spatial structure.
- Avoid circular validation.
- Pair global maps with local visual evidence.
- Make every case study answer a specific claim.
- Keep claims cautious and aligned with evidence.

## What should not transfer automatically

- Do not use CIELAB / U-CIE as the default method in the new project.
- Do not use ΔE as a required landmarkness metric.
- Do not force UrbanHue into the new paper.
- Do not redo the UrbanHue paper under a cognitive-map title.

## When Codex should read UrbanHue archive

- UrbanHue paper revision is needed;
- UrbanHue code, figures, or methods are being updated;
- the user asks to borrow a specific methodological lesson from UrbanHue.
